\section{Proofs}\label{app:proofs}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:joint}]
($\supseteq$) Let $d \in \ker M_c$ and $v = v(d)$ as in
Condition~\ref{cond:S}. For $i \in S_c$, Proposition~\ref{prop:char}(b)
gives $X_i d + v_i z_i = 0$; for $i \notin S_c$, $d \in \ker M_c$ gives
$X_i d = 0$ and $z_i = 0$, so (\ref{eq:L}) holds. Condition~\ref{cond:S}
gives (\ref{eq:T}). Hence $v \in \A_c$.

($\subseteq$) Let $v \in \A_c$ with witness $d$. By (\ref{eq:L}) and
Proposition~\ref{prop:char}(b), $d \in \ker M_c$ and $v_i = -z_i^{\top}X_i d
/ z_i^{\top} z_i$ for $i \in S_c$. For $i \notin S_c$, (\ref{eq:T}) at any
$t$ with $z_i(t) \neq 0$ gives $v_i = -x_i(t)^{\top} d / z_i(t)$, which
by Condition~\ref{cond:S} is $v_i(d)$. So $v = v(d)$.

If $z_i(t) = 0$ for every $t$ as well as at every observation, then
column $c$ of subject $i$'s random effects does not enter the likelihood
at all, $v_i$ is unrestricted, and the corresponding coordinate is
identified by its prior alone; it plays no part in the ridge.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{A sufficient condition for Condition~\ref{cond:S}.} Suppose
each column of the fixed-effects design and column $c$ of the
random-effects design are, for subject $i$, functions $f_j(t; w_i)$ and
$g(t; w_i)$ of time and baseline covariates, and that for each $d$ and
scalar $s$ the function $\sum_j d_j f_j(\cdot\,; w_i) + s\, g(\cdot\,; w_i)$
lies in a linear space of functions that is determined by its values at
subject $i$'s $n_i$ observation times (for example, polynomials of degree
less than $n_i$). Then, for $i \in S_c$, (\ref{eq:L}) at the observation
times implies the identity at all $t$, which is Condition~\ref{cond:S} for
that subject. Subject-constant columns, a linear time column and
covariate-by-time interactions satisfy this with $n_i \geq 2$.
